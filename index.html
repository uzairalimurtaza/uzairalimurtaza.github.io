Principles

Ad-free-first. I ship v1.0 of every app 100% ad-free. Ads come later, and only as an opt-in.

Honest by default. Utilities that estimate something (like sound level) should say so plainly, not pretend to be certified instruments.

Local by default. Data stays on the device unless you explicitly share it. No trackers, no accounts.
