Privacy Policy — Sound Meter

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sound Meter Android app ("the app", "we") handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. The microphone — measuring, and the optional audio clip

Sound Meter uses your device microphone to measure the ambient sound level (loudness) in real time. This is processed on your device, in memory.

Optional audio clip (off by default). If you switch it on, the app records the sound while you measure and saves it as an audio file on your phone only, attached to that saved session, so you can replay it. You can delete it at any time (deleting the session deletes the clip). The app never uploads recordings, and they are left out of Android backup and out of transfers to a new phone. A clip leaves your phone only if you choose to share one yourself: in a saved session, tap Share, then “Share clip”, and pick an app in your phone's share sheet. The share sheet's temporary copy sits in the app's cache, which is cleared at your next share, and Android never backs up the cache.

1a. Camera — optional photo with the dB reading

Only if you open the camera feature does the app ask for camera access. A photo you take is stamped with the reading. The app never uploads photos.

How long a photo is kept. A photo is kept in the app only while it is on the photo screen. Tapping Retake or leaving the screen deletes it, even if you shared it or saved it to the gallery. Only the copies you chose to share or save remain, plus the share sheet's temporary copy in the app's cache, which is cleared at your next share; Android never backs up the cache.

Share. The app passes a photo to another app only when you tap Share and pick that app in your phone's share sheet.

Save to gallery (Android 10 and newer). A photo is copied to your phone's gallery (Pictures/Sound Meter) only if you tap “Save to gallery”. That copy then lives in your phone's shared storage, outside the app, where you manage it like any other picture (your gallery or backup app may treat it like your other photos); the app never uploads it. It stays there until you delete it, even if you uninstall the app. This uses Android's media store and needs no storage permission; on older Android versions the button is not shown.

1b. Saved sessions

Measurement history (levels, times, your notes and place names) is stored on your phone, and the app never uploads it. You can share a session as an image of its result card, a short text summary or a CSV file of its data, through your phone's share sheet, only when you tap Share. We never receive it. If Android backup is on, your sessions can be part of it (Section 1c).

1c. Android backup

The app itself never uploads anything. If you have Android backup turned on, Android's own backup to your Google account may include this app's data, such as your settings and saved sessions, so you get them back on a new phone. The same data can be copied when you move to a new phone with Android's transfer tool. Audio clips and photos are never part of this backup or transfer. You control backup in your phone's settings, and we cannot see your backup.

2. We do not collect personal data

The app has no account, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts or location. The app does not read your photo gallery: it takes a photo only when you use its camera screen, and it adds a photo to your gallery only when you tap “Save to gallery” (Section 1a). Your settings (such as theme and calibration offset) and the last reading shown by the home-screen widget are stored on your phone, and the app never uploads them (for Android backup, see Section 1c).

3. Advertising — none in this version

This version of Sound Meter shows no ads and includes no active advertising SDK data collection. The app collects no personal data at all.

If a future update introduces advertising (Google AdMob), this policy will be updated first, with full disclosure of the data involved, and (for users in the EEA/UK) a consent request will be shown before any ads are served.

4. Permissions we request

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) — required to measure sound level; audio is stored only if you turn on the optional audio clip, and the app stores it only on your phone (Section 1).
  • Camera (CAMERA) — requested only when you open the photo feature (Section 1a).
  • Vibration — for the optional noise alert.
  • Internet / Network state — not used by this version (reserved for a future ads update); the app itself makes no network requests. The privacy policy link in Settings opens in your device's browser, outside the app.

You can revoke the microphone permission at any time in your device Settings. Without it, the app cannot measure sound.

5. Children

The app is rated for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. This version shows no advertising.

6. Data security & retention

Because we don't collect or store your data on any server, there is nothing for us to breach, sell, or retain. On your phone, settings stay until you change them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Saved sessions and their audio clips stay until you delete them (deleting a session deletes its clip), clear the app's data or uninstall the app. A photo stays in the app only while it is on the photo screen; a copy you saved to the gallery stays there until you delete it, even after you uninstall the app (Section 1a). An Android backup copy (Section 1c) is kept by Android in your Google account.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
