Privacy Policy — Prayer Times: Adhan & Qibla

Last updated: 16 November 2026 (version 1.0.0)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Prayer Times: Adhan & Qibla Android app ("Prayer Times" on your home screen; package com.ardesse.prayertimes, "the app", "we"), published under the Ardesse Apps developer name, handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. In short

Prayer Times shows the daily prayer times for your place, alerts you at the times you choose, points to the Qibla and shows the Hijri date. Everything is calculated on your phone, and the app works offline. It has no account and no login. Your location is used only on your phone and is never sent to us or to anyone else. We use no analytics, and this version shows no ads.

2. Your location

  • Why: prayer times, the Qibla direction and the distance to the Kaaba depend on where you are. Your position also lets the app correct the compass from magnetic north to true north, and find the nearest city in the app's built-in city list so it can show its name.
  • How: if you tap "Use my location", Android asks for the location permission. The app reads your position only while you are using it; it never asks for background location. You can refuse and instead search for a city (from a list stored inside the app, so typing a city sends nothing) or enter coordinates by hand.
  • Where it goes: the place you choose (city name, coordinates and time zone) is saved on your phone so the times are ready next time. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

3. Other data stored only on your phone

The app saves these settings in its own private storage on your device:

  • your calculation method, Asr setting (Standard or Hanafi) and high-latitude rule;
  • which prayers alert you, with sound or vibration only, and the optional reminder before each prayer;
  • your language and display choices (for example "Always night");
  • small housekeeping values, such as whether you finished the first-run steps.

This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

5. We do not collect personal data

The app has no account, no sign-up and no login. We (Ardesse Apps) do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts, and we do not collect or store any data from the app. The app contains no analytics and no crash-reporting software, and it makes no network requests of its own.

6. Android backup

If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's settings (including your saved place) in your phone's backup to your Google account, so they come back on a new phone. This is a service of Android and Google that you control in your phone's settings; we cannot see or access these backups.

7. Advertising

This version of the app shows no ads. It contains Google's advertising software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), but in this version that software is switched off: it does not start, does not ask for consent, does not load ads and collects nothing. If a later version shows ads, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe what Google's advertising software collects and the choices you have.

8. Third-party content

  • City list: the app includes a list of cities from GeoNames (geonames.org), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. It is a file inside the app; the app does not connect to GeoNames.
  • Google Play: downloads, updates and (in future) any purchase go through Google Play, under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.

10. Deleting your data

All data stays on your phone. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when a new version adds a feature. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
