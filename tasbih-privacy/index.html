Privacy Policy — Amber Tasbih

Last updated: 29 October 2026 (version 1.5.0)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Amber Tasbih Android app (formerly “Tasbih Counter”) (package com.uzairalimurtaza.tasbih, "the app", "we"), published under the Ardesse Apps developer name, handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. 100% offline — nothing leaves your phone

Amber Tasbih is a free digital tasbih / dhikr counter with prayer times that works entirely offline. It contains no ads, no analytics and no account or login, and since version 1.3.0 it does not even have the Internet permission: it cannot send anything anywhere. Prayer times are calculated on your phone. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

2. Data stored only on your device

To do its job, the app saves the following locally on your device, in a local Hive database:

  • Your tasbih counts and daily counting history
  • Streaks
  • Your own counters and custom dhikr
  • Your reminders and your optional daily intention
  • Your city for prayer times (if you set one) and your prayer settings
  • Your wallpaper choice, and a private copy of your own photo if you pick one as the wallpaper
  • App settings (language, targets, counting and vibration preferences)
  • For the home-screen widget: the dhikr on the counter, its count and today's total, kept in the app's own storage so the widget can show them

This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. The in-app "Delete all local data" option (About & privacy) deletes your counts and targets, your streak and totals, your history and the counters you made, at any time; your settings, reminders, city and wallpaper stay.

3. We do not collect personal data

The app has no account, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or contacts, and we collect no data at all. Two things are optional, stay on your phone, and are never sent anywhere:

  • Your approximate location, only if you tap "Use current location" to set your city for prayer times. The app explains why first, then Android asks you. It takes one approximate reading, finds your nearest city, and keeps only that city and a position rounded to about 1 km. You can always choose your city from the list instead.
  • One photo, only if you choose "Your photo" as the wallpaper. It is picked through Android's photo picker, so the app sees only the photo you choose, and a private copy is kept inside the app.

This matches the app's Google Play Data Safety declaration: "No data collected".

4. Backup file (only when you make one)

About & privacy has "Save a backup file" and "Restore from a file".

  • A backup file is made only when you tap "Save a backup file". It holds your counts, history, your own counters, your reminders, your settings and your city (if you set one); never your wallpaper photo.
  • You choose where it goes: Android shows its own "Save as" screen, where you pick a folder on your phone or a service such as Google Drive. The app itself sends nothing anywhere; anything that happens to the file after you save or share it is up to you.
  • "Restore from a file" reads only the file you pick, and replaces the data in the app after you confirm.

5. Home-screen widget

If you add the Amber Tasbih widget to your home screen, it shows the dhikr on your counter and its count there, so anyone who can see your screen can see it. Tapping the widget counts on your phone only. You can remove the widget at any time by long-pressing it.

6. Advertising — none in this version

This version of Amber Tasbih shows no ads and includes no advertising SDK data collection. The Android Advertising ID permission is explicitly removed from the app.

If a future update introduces advertising, this policy will be updated before that release, with full disclosure of the data involved.

7. Permissions

This is the complete list in the app's Android manifest:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — optional and opt-in, used only for the dhikr reminders you choose. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; no notification data is sent anywhere. Deny or revoke it and the app works normally, just without reminders.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — so your reminders are set again after your phone restarts or the app updates.
  • Approximate location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) — optional, asked only when you tap "Use current location" for prayer times (see section 3). Never precise or background location.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE) — the small vibration as you count, which you can turn off or soften in Settings.
  • Keep the device awake (WAKE_LOCK) and view network status (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — these come with Android's WorkManager library, which is part of the home-screen widget support. Neither lets the app go online: the app has no Internet permission and sends nothing. (Keeping the screen on while you count uses no permission.)
  • An app-internal permission (…DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION) that Android libraries add so that only Amber Tasbih itself can send messages to its own parts. No other app can use it.
  • The photo picker for "Your photo" and the "Save as" / file picker for backups need no permission: the app never sees your other photos or files.

The app has no Internet permission. Links such as this privacy policy open in your device's browser or the Play Store app, outside the app.

8. Children

The app is rated for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. This version shows no advertising.

9. Data security & retention

Because we don't collect or store your data on any server, there is nothing for us to breach, sell, or retain. On-device data remains until you use the in-app "Delete all local data" option, clear the app's data, or uninstall.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date.
