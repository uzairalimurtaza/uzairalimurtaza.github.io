Privacy Policy — Dominoes Offline: Draw & Fives

Last updated: 19 November 2026 (version 1.0.1)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dominoes Offline: Draw & Fives Android app ("Dominoes" on your home screen; package com.ardesse.dominoes, "the app", "we"), published under the Ardesse Apps developer name, handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. In short

Dominoes is an offline game: Draw, Block and All Fives against the computer, or with friends on one phone. Everything runs on your phone. The app has no account and no login. We collect no data. We use no analytics and no crash-reporting software, and this version shows no ads.

2. Data stored only on your phone

The app saves these things in its own private storage on your device:

  • the game in progress, so you can continue it later;
  • your statistics (games played, won and lost, your winning streak);
  • your settings: the table rules, sound, vibration, language, and which rule cards you have already seen;
  • your remaining hints and undos.

This data is never transmitted to us or to anyone else. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

3. We do not collect personal data

The app has no account, no sign-up and no login. We (Ardesse Apps) do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts, and we do not collect or store any data from the app. The computer players run on your phone; no game is sent anywhere.

5. Purchases

This version offers no in-app purchases. Google Play's billing library is still inside the app: when the app starts, it asks Google Play on your phone for your purchases of this app, and the answer stays on your phone. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Advertising

This version of the app shows no ads. It contains Google's advertising software (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), but in this version the app's ad code is left out when the app is built: the advertising software is never started, no consent is requested, no ad is loaded, no advertising ID is declared or read, and nothing is collected.

If a later version can show ads, this is how it will work, and this policy will be updated before that version is released:

  • Ads will have a remote on/off switch. That version will download a small settings file from our own server (cfg.ardesse.com) that says only whether ads are on and how often they may appear. The request is an ordinary web download: it contains no account and no identifier from the app, although, like any web request, the server can see your IP address. The file contains nothing about you.
  • While the switch is off (and whenever the file is missing or unreadable), that version behaves like this one: the advertising software is not started, no consent is requested and no ad is loaded.
  • While the switch is on, the app will first ask for your consent where the law requires it, and only then start Google's advertising software (AdMob), which may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID to show and measure ads. You will be able to change your choice in the app's settings.
  • The limits we publish on the store page apply: no banner ads, no ads during a game, a full-screen ad only after a finished match, and videos for extra hints or undos only when you choose them.

7. Android backup

If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's data (your saved game, statistics and settings) in your phone's backup to your Google account, so it comes back on a new phone. This is a service of Android and Google that you control in your phone's settings; we cannot see or access these backups.

8. Children

The app is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone.

9. Deleting your data

All data stays on your phone. To delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when a new version adds a feature. The date at the top shows the latest version. Important changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
